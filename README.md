**⚡ ZeroHour AI — The Last-Minute Life Saver**

An AI-powered productivity companion that helps you beat deadlines, build habits, and take action when time is running out.

🌐 Live Demo: https://zerohour-ai.onrender.com

---

**🎯 Problem Statement**

Students, professionals, and entrepreneurs frequently miss deadlines, assignments, meetings, and important commitments. Existing tools rely on passive reminders that are easy to ignore and do nothing to help users actually complete tasks.

---

**💡 Solution**

ZeroHour AI is an intelligent productivity companion that proactively helps users plan, prioritize, and complete tasks before deadlines are missed. It goes beyond reminders — it takes action with the user.

---

**✨ Features**

💬 AI Productivity Assistant
Powered by LLaMA 3.3 70B via Groq API. Describe your situation and get an instant personalized action plan with empathetic, structured responses.

⏰ Smart Deadline Tracker
Add tasks with deadlines and get real-time urgency badges — Critical, Due Soon, On Track, or Overdue. Mark tasks complete with checkboxes.

🎯 Goal & Habit Tracker
Add daily or weekly habits, track streaks automatically, and get AI-powered motivational messages.
